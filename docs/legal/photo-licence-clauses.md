# Draft: photo licence wording for the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Status: **draft for legal review**, not published. Shahin chose on 2026-10-15 that a user's liked public trip photo may represent a place across Tripinly, under a clause in the Terms of Service. Tripinly has no published Terms of Service yet; these clauses go into them before launch. A lawyer should check the wording against the law of the launch countries (Austria, Hungary) and the app store rules.

## What the product does

- A trip photo is shown only to the trip's members while the trip is private.
- When the trip is public, anyone can see the photo on that trip.
- When a public photo gets likes from at least 2 people who are not its uploader, the trip owner or the trip's members, it may also be shown as an image of the place it was taken at: on the place card, in the place's photo list and on the map pin. It shows there to anyone using Tripinly.
- It stops showing as soon as the photo is deleted, the trip becomes private, the photo or trip is hidden by moderation, the uploader's account is suspended or deleted, or the photo loses the likes it needs. Viewers who blocked the uploader, or were blocked by them, never see it.
- Anyone can report a photo, including for copyright. An admin reviews the report and can hide or delete the photo.

## Terms of Service: "Your content"

> **Your photos and other content.** You keep all rights you have in the photos, text and other content you add to Tripinly ("your content"). You confirm that you own your content or have permission to share it, and that it doesn't break anyone else's rights.
>
> **The licence you give us.** So that we can run Tripinly, you give us a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, store, copy, resize, re-encode, display and distribute your content within the Tripinly apps and services, to the extent needed to provide them. This includes showing a photo from a **public** trip to anyone using Tripinly, both on that trip and, once other people have liked it, as an image of the place where it was taken (for example on the place's card or its map pin). We don't sell your content and don't use it in advertising outside Tripinly.
>
> **How long it lasts.** The licence ends for each item when you delete it, make its trip private, or delete your account. Copies may stay in backups for a short time until they are overwritten, and content that other people already saved on their own devices is outside our control.
>
> **Reporting.** If you believe content on Tripinly infringes your copyright or other rights, report it in the app (choose "Copyright or ownership") or contact us. We review every report and remove content that infringes.

## Privacy Policy: "What other people see"

> Photos you add to a **public** trip can be seen by anyone using Tripinly. If other people like such a photo, it can also be shown as an image of the place where it was taken, together with your trip. It stops being shown there when you delete the photo, make the trip private or delete your account. Photos on private trips are only visible to the trip's members. We remove location and camera data (EXIF) from every photo before anyone else can see it.

## Notes for the reviewer

- Legal basis: showing a user's content is processing necessary for the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); the licence is the copyright side of the same thing.
- The licence is limited to running the service, so it shouldn't need separate consent; the place display is named explicitly so it is not a surprise (transparency, Art. 13).
- Copyright reports map to EU DSA notice-and-action (Art. 16) and the Austrian/Hungarian copyright acts; the in-app report with reason `copyright` is the notice channel. A contact address for notices from people without an account is still needed before launch.
- Wikimedia Commons images are not user content; they are shown under their own licences with a credit under each image (`10-maps-places-routing.md`).
